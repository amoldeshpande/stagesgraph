The gym in my building has an old Les Mills Stages bike with no subscription or plans to acquire a subscription. The bike is fully functional otherwise and if I pair the power meter with my Garmin watch I can get perfectly useful stats for my workouts. However, I have to keep glancing at my watch to make sure my heart rate is in the target range. It would also be nice to view the output of the power meter.

Now, the table on the bike runs Android 5.1.1 with no way to upgrade it. I haven't written Android apps in years and had no desire to figure out how to do it for such an old device. I asked codex(chat-gpt) to write me an app to show graphs of heart rate and power and it did a really good job in about 2 minutes. It made a mistake related to UTF-8 vs Windows OEM code page initially, but was able to fix it by itself. 

I figure someone else may find this app useful, so here it is. I have no idea what tools, sdks etc. codex used, but in the worst case you can feed this to the AI and have it generate an APK for you.
